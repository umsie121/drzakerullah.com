Fees and insurance

Mr Ullah sees insured and self-funding patients at each of his clinics. Here is how payment and authorisation work.

Insured

Mr Ullah is recognised by the major UK private medical insurers and is fee-assured with the principal insurers, so you should not face a shortfall for his fees.

With Bupa he is a Platinum consultant, fee-assured, and part of Bupa’s Open Referral network. If your GP gives an open referral, ask Bupa for Mr Md Z Ullah by name, or choose Nuffield Health The Holly in Buckhurst Hill.

Before your first appointment, most insurers ask for two things: a GP referral, and a pre-authorisation number, which you obtain by calling your insurer with the referral. Bring the number to your appointment.

Self-funding

You do not need insurance or a referral. Book with the hospital or booking service you prefer, and the consultation fee is confirmed when you book. If you need tests or surgery, you receive a written, fixed price before anything is arranged, so there are no surprises.

What is usually charged separately

  • Imaging, such as mammogram or ultrasound, charged by the hospital
  • Biopsy and laboratory (pathology) fees
  • For surgery: the hospital stay, theatre and anaesthetist, usually combined in one fixed-price package

Prices vary between hospitals and services, which is why they are quoted by each partner rather than listed here. The booking page links to each one, and the private secretary can tell you what is included before you commit.

Questions

Questions about fees

Do I need a GP referral if I am insured?

Most insurers ask for a GP referral before they authorise a specialist appointment. Some accept a referral from a digital GP service included in your policy. Check with your insurer.

Is Mr Ullah fee-assured?

Yes. Mr Ullah is a Bupa Platinum consultant and fee-assured with Bupa, and is recognised by the other major UK insurers, so you should not face a shortfall for his fees. Confirm your own cover with your insurer.

How much does a consultation cost if I am self-funding?

Consultation fees are confirmed by the hospital or booking service when you book, before you attend. Imaging and biopsies are usually charged separately, and for tests or surgery you receive a written, fixed price before you commit.

Is breast reconstruction covered?

Reconstruction after breast cancer, including surgery to the other breast for symmetry, is normally covered by insurers as part of cancer treatment. Cosmetic procedures generally are not.
