Questions

Seeing Mr Ullah at Canary Wharf

How do I book with Mr Ullah at Bupa Wharfside Health Centre?

Call his private secretary on 020 8936 1263, send an enquiry through this website, or ask your GP to refer you to Mr Md Zaker Ullah at Bupa Wharfside Health Centre. Insured patients should obtain a pre-authorisation number from their insurer first.

Where is Bupa Wharfside Health Centre?

Bupa Wharfside Health Centre is at 50 Bank Street, London E14 5NT. On Bank Street in Canary Wharf, a few minutes' walk from Canary Wharf station on the Jubilee line and the Elizabeth line, and close to the DLR.

Which conditions does Mr Ullah treat here?

New breast lumps and breast pain, nipple discharge, breast cancer surgery, reconstruction, breast reduction, fat grafting, gynaecomastia, and general surgery including hernia and gallbladder operations. Some operations take place only at particular hospitals; the secretary will advise.

Can I be seen quickly with a new breast symptom?

Yes. New breast symptoms are given priority, with the aim of examination, imaging and any biopsy at a single visit.
