Breast surgeon in Buckhurst Hill, for Woodford Green, Loughton and Chigwell

Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital is one of Mr Ullah's three private clinics, and the home of his private secretary. A calm and well equipped hospital on the border of London and Essex, it is a few minutes from Woodford Green, Loughton and Chigwell, and serves patients from across Essex, Hertfordshire and north east London. Mr Ullah runs its Rapid Access Breast Clinic on Tuesday afternoons, with consultation and imaging on the same day.

Questions

Seeing Mr Ullah at The Holly, Essex

Is there a private breast surgeon near Woodford Green?

Yes. Mr Ullah consults at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital on the High Road in Buckhurst Hill, a few minutes from Woodford Green, South Woodford, Loughton and Chigwell, with parking on site. His Rapid Access Breast Clinic runs on Tuesday afternoons.

Can I see Mr Ullah at The Holly if I am an NHS patient at Whipps Cross?

Yes, if you choose to be seen privately, either through insurance or as a self-funding patient. Mr Ullah leads the breast unit at Whipps Cross and many patients from the area see him at The Holly.

How do I book with Mr Ullah at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital?

Call his private secretary on 020 8936 1263, send an enquiry through this website, or ask your GP to refer you to Mr Md Zaker Ullah at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital. Insured patients should obtain a pre-authorisation number from their insurer first.

Where is Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital?

Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital is at High Road, Buckhurst Hill IG9 5HX. On the High Road in Buckhurst Hill at the edge of Epping Forest, with parking on site. Buckhurst Hill Central line station is close by, and the M11 and M25 are a few minutes away.

Which conditions does Mr Ullah treat here?

New breast lumps and breast pain, nipple discharge, breast cancer surgery, reconstruction, breast reduction, fat grafting, gynaecomastia, and general surgery including hernia and gallbladder operations. Some operations take place only at particular hospitals; the secretary will advise.

Can I be seen quickly with a new breast symptom?

Yes. New breast symptoms are given priority, with the aim of examination, imaging and any biopsy at a single visit.
