Questions

Seeing Mr Ullah at City of London

Is there a private breast surgeon in the City of London?

Yes. Mr Ullah sees patients at Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's Hospital, 38 Giltspur Street, London EC1A 7BS, close to Farringdon, Barbican, St Paul's and Liverpool Street.

Can I be seen near Liverpool Street or Moorgate?

Yes. Nuffield Health St Bartholomew's is a short journey from both, and from Farringdon on the Elizabeth line, which also links it directly with Canary Wharf.

How do I book with Mr Ullah at Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's Hospital?

Call his private secretary on 020 8936 1263, send an enquiry through this website, or ask your GP to refer you to Mr Md Zaker Ullah at Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's Hospital. Insured patients should obtain a pre-authorisation number from their insurer first.

Where is Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's Hospital?

Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's Hospital is at 38 Giltspur Street, London EC1A 7BS. Inside the St Bartholomew's Hospital site, a few minutes on foot from Farringdon, Barbican and St Paul's, and easily reached from Canary Wharf on the Elizabeth line.

Which conditions does Mr Ullah treat here?

New breast lumps and breast pain, nipple discharge, breast cancer surgery, reconstruction, breast reduction, fat grafting, gynaecomastia, and general surgery including hernia and gallbladder operations. Some operations take place only at particular hospitals; the secretary will advise.

Can I be seen quickly with a new breast symptom?

Yes. New breast symptoms are given priority. The aim is examination, imaging and any biopsy at a single visit.
