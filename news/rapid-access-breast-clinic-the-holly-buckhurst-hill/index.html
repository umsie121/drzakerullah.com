The Rapid Access Breast Clinic at The Holly, Buckhurst Hill

A guide to the private Rapid Access Breast Clinic at Nuffield Health The Holly, Buckhurst Hill: booking, tests on the day, results and getting there.

By Mr Md Zaker Ullah FRCS, Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon · Medically reviewed 3 November 2026

In short: the Rapid Access Breast Clinic at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital in Buckhurst Hill is a private one-stop clinic. You see a consultant breast surgeon, have any scans you need and, if necessary, a biopsy — usually in a single afternoon. I run it on Tuesday afternoons.

Many of my patients come from Loughton, Chigwell, Woodford, Epping and across north-east London and Essex. This is what they can expect.

Before you come

  • If you are insured, ask your GP for a short referral letter and call your insurer for a pre-authorisation number. Most insurers — including Bupa, AXA Health, Aviva and Vitality — require both.
  • If you are self-funding, you can book directly without a referral. The hospital confirms the consultation fee when you book.
  • Bring any previous mammogram or ultrasound reports, a list of medications and, if you have one, your referral letter.
  • Avoid deodorant or talcum powder on the day if you may need a mammogram.

On the day

  1. Consultation. I take a full history — your symptom, your family history, any medications — and examine both breasts and armpits. There is time to ask questions.
  2. Imaging. Depending on your age and symptoms, you will have an ultrasound, a mammogram or both, usually in the same visit.
  3. Biopsy if needed. If anything needs a tissue sample, a needle biopsy is taken under local anaesthetic, guided by ultrasound. It takes a few minutes.
  4. A plan before you leave. Most patients leave either reassured or with a clear next step and a date for biopsy results.

This combination of examination, imaging and biopsy is called triple assessment. It is the gold standard for any new breast symptom.

Results

Imaging results are usually explained on the day. Biopsy results normally take a few working days, and I see you again or call you to go through them. If anything needs treatment, the plan moves forward without changing hands. The surgeon who examined you is the surgeon who treats you.

Getting there

The Holly is on the High Road in Buckhurst Hill, on the edge of Epping Forest, with on-site parking. Buckhurst Hill Central line station is nearby, and the M11 and M25 are minutes away. Full clinic details.

If you have a new breast symptom and would rather be seen in central London, I also consult at The Harley Street Clinic and Nuffield Health at St Bartholomew's.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Rapid Access Breast Clinic appointment take?

Allow around one to two hours. That leaves time for the consultation, imaging and, if needed, a biopsy at the same visit.

Can I go to the breast clinic at The Holly without a GP referral?

Yes, if you are paying for yourself. Insured patients usually need a GP referral and a pre-authorisation number from their insurer.

Will I get my results on the same day?

Examination and scan findings are usually explained on the day. Biopsy results typically take a few working days.

This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have a breast symptom, arrange an assessment through your GP or a specialist clinic. In an emergency call 999.
