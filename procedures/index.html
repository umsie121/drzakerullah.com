Areas of practice

The diagnosis and surgical treatment of breast disease, benign and malignant, with oncoplastic and reconstructive surgery, the male breast, and keyhole general surgery.

01

Breast symptoms

A new lump, pain or change, assessed quickly at a one-stop clinic.

  • Breast lumps and painExamination, imaging and biopsy for a new breast symptom, usually at a single visit.→
02

Breast cancer

Breast conserving surgery, mastectomy and lymph node surgery, planned with the result in mind.

  • Breast cancer surgeryBreast conserving surgery, therapeutic mammoplasty, mastectomy and lymph node surgery.→
03

Reconstruction and reshaping

Rebuilding, reducing and refining the breast, during or after cancer treatment.

  • Breast reconstructionImmediate and delayed reconstruction with implants, your own tissue, chest wall perforator flaps and fat grafting.→
  • Breast reductionReduction mammoplasty for comfort and proportion, or to balance the breasts after cancer surgery.→
  • Fat graftingUsing your own fat to refine shape after surgery, correct contour and soften scar tissue.→
04

Men's and general surgery

The male breast, keyhole hernia and gallbladder surgery, and skin lumps.

  • GynaecomastiaSpecialist assessment of the male breast, and surgery for established gynaecomastia.→
  • Hernia and gallbladderLaparoscopic gallbladder removal, hernia repair and appendicectomy.→
  • Lumps, cysts and molesRemoval of lipomas, sebaceous cysts, moles and other skin lesions, usually under local anaesthetic.→
