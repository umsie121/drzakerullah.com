Treatment 04

Breast reduction and symmetrisation

Reduction mammoplasty reshapes heavy breasts into a lighter, higher and more comfortable form: for relief of symptoms, for proportion, or to balance the breasts after cancer surgery.

Written and medically reviewed by Mr Md Zaker Ullah FRCS. Updated 4 November 2026.

Who benefits

Heavy breasts are a physical burden. Women describe pain in the neck, shoulders and back, grooves from bra straps, irritated skin beneath the breast and real limits on exercise. A reduction removes excess tissue and skin, lifts the breast and repositions the nipple. The scars fade considerably over the following one to two years.

Symmetry after cancer surgery

As an oncoplastic surgeon, much of Mr Ullah's reduction work is symmetrisation: reducing, lifting or reshaping one breast so that it matches a breast treated for cancer or reconstructed. It is a routine part of the reconstructive pathway and is usually covered by insurers as part of cancer treatment.

The operation and recovery

A breast reduction is performed under general anaesthetic, takes around two to three hours, and is done as a day case or with one night in hospital. Most women return to desk work within one to two weeks and to full exercise at about six weeks. Before any decision, Mr Ullah talks plainly about scar patterns, nipple sensation and the effect on breastfeeding.

Questions

Questions about breast reduction

Will breast reduction affect nipple sensation or breastfeeding?

Both can be affected. Some change in nipple sensation is common at first and usually improves, although it can be permanent. Many women can breastfeed after a reduction, but it cannot be guaranteed. If future breastfeeding matters to you, say so, because it influences technique and timing.

What scars does a breast reduction leave?

Usually a scar around the areola, a vertical line down to the fold beneath the breast, and often a line within the fold itself, known as the anchor pattern. Scars are red and firm at first and fade considerably over 12 to 24 months.

Is breast reduction covered by insurance?

Cosmetic reduction generally is not. Symmetrisation after breast cancer surgery is usually treated as part of reconstruction. Your insurer will confirm cover in advance.

How long is recovery after breast reduction?

Most women are back at desk work within one to two weeks, driving at around two weeks, and exercising fully at about six weeks. A supportive bra is worn day and night for several weeks.
