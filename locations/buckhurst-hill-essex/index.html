A calm, well-equipped hospital on the London–Essex border, it serves patients from across Essex, Hertfordshire and north-east London, and runs a Rapid Access Breast Clinic with Mr Ullah on Tuesday afternoons.

FAQ

Seeing Mr Ullah at The Holly, Essex

How do I book an appointment with Mr Ullah at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital?

Call Mr Ullah's private secretary on 020 8936 1263, send an enquiry through this website, or ask your GP to refer you to Mr Md Zaker Ullah at Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital. Insured patients should get a pre-authorisation number from their insurer first.

Where is Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital?

Nuffield Health The Holly Hospital is at High Road, Buckhurst Hill, IG9 5HX. On the High Road in Buckhurst Hill on the edge of Epping Forest, with on-site parking and Buckhurst Hill Central line station nearby. Easily reached from the M11 and M25.

Which conditions does Mr Ullah treat here?

New breast lumps and breast pain, nipple discharge, breast cancer surgery, reconstruction, breast reduction and augmentation, fat grafting, gynaecomastia and general surgery including hernia and gallbladder operations. Some operations are only performed at certain hospitals; the secretary will advise.

Can I be seen quickly with a new breast symptom?

Yes. New breast symptoms are prioritised, and the aim is examination, imaging and any biopsy at a single visit.
