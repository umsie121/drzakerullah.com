Areas of practice

Diagnosis and surgical treatment of breast disease, both benign and malignant, with oncoplastic and reconstructive surgery and a laparoscopic general surgical practice.

Most breast symptoms turn out to be benign — but every new symptom deserves a proper answer, quickly.

Breast cancer surgery

Surgery is usually the cornerstone of breast cancer treatment.

LumpectomyMastectomySentinel nodeOncoplastic

Breast reconstruction

Reconstruction restores the shape of the breast after cancer surgery — at the same operation or years later.

ImmediateDelayedLD flapImplant
04

Breast reduction

Reduction mammoplasty reshapes heavy breasts to a lighter, higher, more comfortable form — for relief of symptoms, for proportion, or to balance the breasts after cancer surgery..

ReductionUpliftSymmetry
05

Breast augmentation

Augmentation uses an implant to increase the size or restore the fullness of the breast.

ImplantsSymmetry
06

Fat grafting (lipofilling)

Fat grafting — lipofilling — moves a small amount of your own fat, harvested by gentle liposuction, into the breast to smooth contours, fill defects and soften scarred tissue..

LipofillingContour

Gynaecomastia (men)

Enlargement of male breast tissue is common, benign in the vast majority of cases, and very treatable.

MenLiposuctionGland excision

Hernia & gallbladder
