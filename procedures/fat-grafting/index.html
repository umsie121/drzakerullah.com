Reconstruction and reshaping

Fat grafting to the breast

Fat grafting, also called lipofilling, moves a small amount of your own fat into the breast by gentle liposuction, to smooth contours, fill defects and soften scarred tissue. Mr Ullah introduced the technique at Whipps Cross.

What it is used for

In breast surgery, fat grafting is above all a refining technique. It fills a dent left by breast conserving surgery, smooths the edges of an implant reconstruction, softens tissue affected by radiotherapy and corrects small differences between the breasts. No foreign material is used, and the marks left at the donor and treated sites are tiny.

How it works

Fat is taken by gentle liposuction, usually from the abdomen or thighs, then purified and placed in the breast in fine threads so that each droplet gains a blood supply. A proportion of the grafted fat, commonly 30 to 50 per cent, is naturally absorbed over the first few months. For that reason larger corrections are often carried out over two or three sessions.

Recovery

Fat grafting is usually a day case. Bruising where the fat was taken is the main after effect, and most patients return to normal activity within a few days. The final result is assessed at around three months, once swelling has settled.

Questions

Questions about fat grafting

Is fat grafting safe after breast cancer?

Current evidence has not shown that fat grafting increases the risk of cancer returning, and it is widely used in reconstruction. Grafted fat can produce benign changes on mammograms, such as oil cysts or calcification, which specialists recognise. Always tell your imaging team that you have had fat grafting.

How many sessions will I need?

Small refinements often need a single session. Larger corrections usually need two or three sessions about three months apart, because some of each graft is absorbed.

Does the transferred fat behave like normal fat?

Yes. Once established, grafted fat is living tissue. It feels natural and will gain and lose volume with your weight, like fat elsewhere.
