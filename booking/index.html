Enquiry

Request an appointment

Your enquiry opens in your email app, addressed to Mr Ullah's private secretary. Please do not include detailed medical information; that is best discussed in consultation.

Insured patients

Mr Ullah is fee-assured with the major UK insurers. Most insurers need a GP referral and a pre-authorisation number before your first appointment.

BupaBupa GlobalAXA HealthAvivaVitalityWPACignaHealixHealth-on-Line

Self-funding patients

You do not need insurance or a referral. The hospital confirms the consultation fee when you book, and gives a written, fixed price for any tests or surgery before you commit.

What to bring

  • GP referral letter (if you have one)
  • Previous breast imaging or biopsy results
  • A list of current medications
  • Insurance details and pre-authorisation number
Urgent symptoms: if you are acutely unwell, call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department.
