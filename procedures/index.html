Treatments

Areas of practice

The diagnosis and surgical treatment of breast disease, benign and malignant, with oncoplastic and reconstructive surgery, the male breast, and keyhole general surgery.

Index
  1. 01Breast lumps and painExamination, imaging and biopsy for a new breast symptom, usually at a single visit.→
  2. 02Breast cancer surgeryBreast conserving surgery, therapeutic mammoplasty, mastectomy and lymph node surgery.→
  3. 03Breast reconstructionImmediate and delayed reconstruction using implants, your own tissue, fat grafting and nipple reconstruction.→
  4. 04Breast reductionReduction mammoplasty for comfort and proportion, or to balance the breasts after cancer surgery.→
  5. 05Breast augmentationImplant augmentation for size or shape, and to restore symmetry after cancer surgery.→
  6. 06Fat graftingUsing your own fat to refine shape after surgery, correct contour and soften scar tissue.→
  7. 07GynaecomastiaSpecialist assessment of the male breast, and surgery for established gynaecomastia.→
  8. 08Hernia and gallbladderLaparoscopic gallbladder removal, hernia repair and appendicectomy.→

Not sure where to begin? Start with a consultation. The right operation, and whether to operate at all, follows from the diagnosis.
