## Getting to The Holly from Loughton, Chigwell, Epping and Theydon Bois

The Holly is on the High Road in Buckhurst Hill, IG9 5HX, on the edge of Epping Forest. Most people from these towns drive, but the Central line is a good option too.

- **From Loughton and Debden.** The High Road in Loughton becomes the High Road in Buckhurst Hill as you head south, so for many Loughton patients the hospital is a few minutes away without leaving the main road. By Tube, Buckhurst Hill is one stop from Loughton.
- **From Chigwell.** It is a short drive across the Roding valley. If you prefer the Tube, Chigwell is on the Hainault branch of the Central line, which joins the main line at Woodford. You can change there for Buckhurst Hill, though for most people from Chigwell the car is quicker.
- **From Theydon Bois and Epping.** Both are further out but still close. The drive runs through or alongside the forest, and Theydon Bois and Epping stations are on the same branch of the Central line as Buckhurst Hill, so there is no change needed.
- **From further afield.** The M11 and M25 are a few minutes from the hospital, which helps patients coming from Harlow, Waltham Abbey, Ongar or Brentwood.

The full address and a map are on my [Buckhurst Hill clinic page](~/locations/buckhurst-hill-essex/).

## Parking and arriving at the hospital

There is parking on site at The Holly. For a clinic appointment, that is usually the easiest option, and it means you are not watching a parking meter while waiting for a scan.

A few things make the day go more smoothly:

- **Allow time for the whole visit.** If you are coming to the Tuesday afternoon clinic for a new symptom, plan on being at the hospital for an hour or two. That leaves room for a scan, and a biopsy if one is needed, at the same visit.
- **You can usually drive yourself home after a clinic visit.** A consultation, a scan and a needle biopsy under local anaesthetic do not normally stop you driving. Some patients still bring a partner, friend or grown-up son or daughter, more for company than transport, and you are welcome to.
- **Bring the paperwork.** If you are insured, bring your GP referral letter and your insurer's pre-authorisation number. Bring any previous mammogram or ultrasound reports, and a list of your medicines.
- **On the day of a possible mammogram,** avoid deodorant or talcum powder, as these can show up on the images.

I consult in English, Bangla and Arabic. For some families, particularly when an older relative is the patient, that makes the conversation easier for everyone.

## What a breast clinic in Loughton's local hospital can do for you

People sometimes ask whether a hospital in Buckhurst Hill can offer the same as a large central London centre. For breast care, The Holly has what most patients need in one place: outpatient clinics, imaging and full operating theatres.

That means most of the care for a new breast symptom can happen close to home:

1. **Assessment.** A consultation and examination with me, followed by an ultrasound, a mammogram or both, depending on your age and what I find. If anything needs sampling, a needle biopsy is taken under local anaesthetic. Together these are called triple assessment, the UK standard for assessing [breast lumps, pain and other benign breast conditions](~/procedures/benign-breast-conditions/).
2. **Results.** Scan findings are usually explained before you leave. Biopsy results normally take a few working days, and I go through them with you myself.
3. **Treatment, if needed.** Most breast symptoms turn out to be benign and need no operation. Where treatment helps, such as removing a fibroadenoma or another benign lump, it can usually be done at The Holly. If a biopsy shows cancer, [breast cancer surgery](~/procedures/breast-cancer-surgery/) can also be carried out there, after the case has been discussed by a multidisciplinary team of radiologists, pathologists, oncologists and specialist nurses.

Having surgery locally matters more than people expect. After a general anaesthetic you will need someone to drive you home, and it is far easier for a family member to collect you from Buckhurst Hill than from central London. Follow-up visits, wound checks and conversations about results are also simpler when the hospital is ten or fifteen minutes away rather than an hour.

The Holly is one of my three private clinics, and they are equal in what I offer. I also consult at Bupa Wharfside Health Centre in Canary Wharf and at Nuffield Health St Bartholomew's in the City.

## Booking from Loughton, Chigwell or Epping

My private secretary is based at The Holly, so for local patients that is usually the simplest route. It is the best one if you have questions about insurance, want a particular day, or are not sure whether to be seen in Buckhurst Hill or in London.

You can also book through the hospital directly on 020 8505 3311, online through Doctify or Top Doctors, or through Bupa Finder if you are a Bupa member. All the routes are on my [booking page](~/booking/).

If you have private medical insurance, ring your insurer before you book. Most will want a GP referral and a pre-authorisation number. I am a Bupa Platinum consultant, fee-assured and part of Bupa's Open Referral network, so Bupa members should not face a shortfall on my fees for covered treatment. I am also recognised by other major insurers, including AXA Health, Aviva, Vitality and WPA. If you are paying for yourself, you do not need a referral, and the hospital confirms the consultation fee when you book. The details are on my [fees and insurance page](~/fees-and-insurance/).

When you call, say that you have a new breast symptom. New symptoms are given priority.

:::note
If you notice a new lump, a change in the skin of the breast or a change in the nipple, please do not wait for a convenient time to be seen. See your GP or book promptly. Most breast symptoms turn out to be benign, but each one deserves a proper assessment.
:::
