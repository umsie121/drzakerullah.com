Questions

Seeing Mr Ullah at Harley Street

How do I book with Mr Ullah at The Harley Street Clinic?

Call his private secretary on 020 8936 1263, send an enquiry through this website, or ask your GP to refer you to Mr Md Zaker Ullah at The Harley Street Clinic. Insured patients should obtain a pre-authorisation number from their insurer first.

Where is The Harley Street Clinic?

The Harley Street Clinic is at 35 Weymouth Street, London W1G 8BJ. A short walk from Regent's Park, Great Portland Street and Oxford Circus stations, in London's historic medical district.

Which conditions does Mr Ullah treat here?

New breast lumps and breast pain, nipple discharge, breast cancer surgery, reconstruction, breast reduction, fat grafting, gynaecomastia, and general surgery including hernia and gallbladder operations. Some operations take place only at particular hospitals; the secretary will advise.

Can I be seen quickly with a new breast symptom?

Yes. New breast symptoms are given priority, with the aim of examination, imaging and any biopsy at a single visit.
