Patients

Your first visit

What to expect from a first appointment with Mr Ullah, from booking to the plan you leave with.

Book a consultation
Four stages

  1. Booking

    Call the private secretary or send an enquiry. If you are insured, obtain a GP referral and a pre-authorisation number from your insurer.

  2. Consultation

    A full history and examination with Mr Ullah himself, with time to talk and to ask questions.

  3. Assessment

    Ultrasound, mammography and needle biopsy at the same visit where needed. This is triple assessment.

  4. The plan

    Results and a written plan. If surgery is needed, the surgeon who examined you is the surgeon who operates.

Bring with you
  • Your GP referral letter, if you have one
  • Any previous breast imaging or biopsy results
  • A list of the medicines you take
  • Your insurance details and pre-authorisation number
  • A list of your questions

If you may need a mammogram, avoid deodorant and talcum powder on the day.

Afterwards

Imaging findings are usually explained on the day. Biopsy results normally take a few working days, after which Mr Ullah sees you again or telephones to explain them. If treatment is needed, it is planned with you and, for cancer, with a multidisciplinary team.

With your consent, Mr Ullah writes to your GP after each consultation. You can read about fees and insurance or see every common question.

Questions

Questions about your visit

How long is the first appointment?

Allow up to an hour and a half if you have a new breast symptom, so that imaging and any biopsy can take place at the same visit. Follow-up appointments are shorter.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, and many patients do. A relative or friend can help you remember what was said and ask the questions you may forget.

Will my GP be told?

With your consent, Mr Ullah writes to your GP after each consultation so that your medical record stays complete.

Can I have a chaperone?

Yes. A chaperone is always available for examinations, and you can ask for one at any point.
