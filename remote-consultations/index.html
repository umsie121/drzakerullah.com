Remote and international

Video consultations and second opinions from abroad

Mr Ullah offers video and telephone consultations, and second opinions for patients anywhere in the UK or overseas, in English, Bangla or Arabic.

Arrange a video consultation
How it works

  1. Enquire

    Contact the private secretary. A video or telephone consultation can usually be arranged within 24 to 48 hours.

  2. Send your records

    Clinic letters, imaging and pathology reports, in English or translated, and the images if you can.

  3. Talk it through

    A consultation with Mr Ullah by secure video, with time to ask every question.

  4. Your written opinion

    A clear letter explaining his view and recommended next steps, for you and your own doctors.

Good to know

A remote consultation is ideal for a second opinion, for discussing results or treatment options, and for follow up. It cannot replace an examination, so if Mr Ullah needs to examine you or arrange scans or a biopsy, he will say so and a face to face appointment can be made at The Holly in Buckhurst Hill, Canary Wharf or St Bartholomew’s.

For patients travelling from abroad for treatment, the practice can help you plan the timing of consultations, tests and surgery. Read more about second opinions.

Questions

Questions about remote consultations

Can I have a consultation with Mr Ullah by video?

Yes. A video or telephone consultation can usually be arranged within 24 to 48 hours of your enquiry. If an examination or tests are needed, a face to face appointment is arranged afterwards.

Can I get a second opinion from abroad?

Yes. Mr Ullah reviews your reports and images, discusses them with you by video, and gives a written opinion that you can share with your own doctors. Treatment, if you choose it, takes place at one of his UK clinics.

What should I send before a remote second opinion?

Clinic letters, imaging reports and, where possible, the images themselves, biopsy or pathology reports, and a list of your medicines. Reports should be in English or come with a translation.

Which languages does Mr Ullah consult in?

English, Bangla and Arabic. Many patients and families find it easier to discuss a diagnosis in their first language. বাংলা · العربية

How much does a remote consultation cost?

The fee is confirmed by the practice when you book, before the consultation takes place. Some UK insurers cover remote consultations; check with yours.
